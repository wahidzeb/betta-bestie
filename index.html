About Betta Bestie

Betta Bestie is a gentle virtual pet game. A little betta fish lives in a round bowl in a cosy room, and you name her, pick her colour and pop in to feed her, keep her water sparkling and play little mini-games together.

She never gets sick and never runs away. If you forget her for a few days she is just a bit hungry and says she missed you, which makes it a calm game for younger players and short daily visits.

3 quick tips

  1. Visit once a day rather than for a long time. A quick feed, a wipe of the glass and one mini-game keeps every meter healthy.
  2. Tap the water to drop a few pellets, but do not overfeed. Leftover pellets make the water dirty.
  3. Open the daily gift first so you never miss a day of the gift calendar.
